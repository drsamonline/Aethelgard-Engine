# Aethelgard — icon prompts — points of interest

The 35 places you can visit on the region maps. They currently borrow the art of whatever lives there, or an emoji.

**35 icons. Generate each at 1:1, on a completely plain flat solid magenta
background** — that is what lets me cut them out. No floor, no shadow, no scene,
no second colour. Save each under the exact filename in its heading.

Everything here is optional: skip any icon and that entry keeps the emoji it
shows today. Send them in any batch size.

---

### Where these go

`poi_<region>_<id>.png` becomes `poi.<region>.<id>` — e.g. `poi_oakhaven_forge.png` fills `poi.oakhaven.forge`.

Five regions share the same three icons (an exit signpost appears in the Cave, Temple and Depths), so `poi_cave_leave.png`, `poi_temple_leave.png` and `poi_depths_leave.png` can be the same drawing saved three times.
