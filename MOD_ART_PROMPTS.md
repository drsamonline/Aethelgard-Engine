# Aethelgard — art prompts for the new mods

Prompts for the art the two new mods can use. Everything here is **optional**:
skip any of it and the mod keeps the art it borrows today (the new foes borrow
existing creature art, the villains borrow their own boss art, and the heroes
borrow the class art). Nothing breaks.

**28 images.** You can send them in any batch size.

## The two rules

**Characters and items** sit on a completely plain flat **magenta** background.
That is what lets me cut them out. No floor, no shadow, no scenery, no second
colour. Generate at **1:1**.

**Backdrops** are a full scene, **no magenta**, generated at **16:9**.

Filenames matter — they become the sprite key. Save each image under the exact
name in its heading, e.g. `enemy_frost_revenant.png`.

## What arrives where

| filename pattern | becomes | used by |
|---|---|---|
| `enemy_*.png` | `enemy.*` | combat, bestiary, codex |
| `class_*.png` | `class.*` and `player.*` | creation screen, hero sheet |
| `bg_*.png` | `bg.*` | region backdrops, title screen |
| `loc_*.png` | `loc.*` | world-map pins |
| `poi_<region>_<id>.png` | `poi.<region>.<id>` | local-map points of interest |

Drop the files in `mods/ai-art/sprites/` and tell me — I will import them, cut
out the magenta, pack them into the atlas and wire them up.
