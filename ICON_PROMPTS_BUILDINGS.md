# Aethelgard — icon prompts — settlement buildings

The 7 buildings you can raise in your settlement. They appear in the Home view.

**7 icons. Generate each at 1:1, on a completely plain flat solid magenta
background** — that is what lets me cut them out. No floor, no shadow, no scene,
no second colour. Save each under the exact filename in its heading.

Everything here is optional: skip any icon and that entry keeps the emoji it
shows today. Send them in any batch size.

---

### Where these go

`bld_<key>.png` becomes `bld.<key>` — e.g. `bld_farm.png` fills `bld.farm`.
