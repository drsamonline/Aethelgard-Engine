# Aethelgard — icon prompts — gear sets

The 5 armour sets. Each shows as a single emblem in the Hero view when you are wearing pieces of it.

**5 icons. Generate each at 1:1, on a completely plain flat solid magenta
background** — that is what lets me cut them out. No floor, no shadow, no scene,
no second colour. Save each under the exact filename in its heading.

Everything here is optional: skip any icon and that entry keeps the emoji it
shows today. Send them in any batch size.

---

### Where these go

`set_<key>.png` becomes `set.<key>` — e.g. `set_ironclad.png` fills `set.ironclad`.
