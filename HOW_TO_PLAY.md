# How to play Aethelgard

## Getting it running — read this first

**Extract the whole folder before you open anything.**

1. Right-click `Aethelgard-Chronicles-of-Fate.zip` → **Extract All** (Windows) or **Extract Here** (Mac).
2. Open the extracted folder — you should see `index.html`, and folders called `js`, `mods`, `icons`.
3. Open **`index.html`** from inside that folder. That's the game.

**Do not open `index.html` from inside the zip viewer.** Most zip tools unpack only the single
file you double-clicked, leaving the `js/` and `mods/` folders behind — the page then loads but
the game cannot start. If that happens the game now tells you so on screen, with these same
instructions.

If you have played before and it looks stuck on an old version, do a **hard refresh**
(Ctrl+F5, or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac). The game updates its own cache automatically from then on.

# How to Play — Aethelgard: Chronicles of Fate

*A game by Dr. Sohil Momin*

---

## Getting started

Open `index.html`, pick a save slot, choose a difficulty (**Story**, **Normal**, **Hard**, **Brutal**), then **create your hero**: a name, one of eight **races**, a gender, and one of eight **classes**.

Your race and class give permanent bonuses — an Orc Berserker is a bruiser; an Elf Archer is a precise killer; a Revenant drains life. Your class also unlocks its own skill set and its own mission line, so different classes play genuinely differently.

## The basics

Three resources regenerate as you play:

- ❤️ **HP** — at 0 you are slain and wake in Oakhaven, 10% lighter in gold.
- ⚡ **Energy** — spent on gathering, exploring, fighting, skills and activities.
- 🧠 **Nerve** — the currency of the Underworld.

**Gold** buys things. **XP** fills the bar; each level grants **+3 AP**, **+1 SP**, **+20 HP**, **+8 energy** and **+2 nerve**, plus a full heal.

## Combat

Fights are turn-based and may involve several enemies. **Tap an enemy card to target it.**

- **⚔️ Attack** — physical damage. Can crit (and double-strike, for a Duel Wielder).
- **🛡️ Defend** — halves the next enemy hit. Use it when you see **⚠️**.
- **🧪 Potion / ⚗️ Draught / 🧴 Antidote** — heal, restore energy, cure afflictions.
- **🏃 Flee** — escape chance from Finesse, Stealth and boots. Bosses cannot be fled.
- **Your skills** — class abilities with energy costs and cooldowns.

**Elements matter.** Enemies resist some elements and are weak to others (50% more damage). **Status effects** (bleed, poison, burn, stun, weaken, ward, rage, regen, focus, mark) apply to both sides — watch the chips.

**Telegraphed attacks:** when an enemy winds up a heavy blow, Defend or stun it to cancel it.

## Progression

- **Hero tab** — spend AP on Might, Finesse, Sorcery, Vitality. See all your combat stats, equipment (7 slots), gear bag, and reputation.
- **Skills tab** — spend SP, or practise a skill for 5⚡ for a free chance to level it. Class skills unlock at set levels.
- **Gear** — items drop or are forged with rarity (common → **mythic**) and affixes. Equip from your bag, and upgrade equipped gear to **+5** with iron and gold.

## The Atlas

Two maps: a **world map** (click a discovered region to travel) and a **local map** for each region, with clickable points of interest.

- **Oakhaven** — market, forge, inn, tavern, arena, fields.
- **Crimson Mire** — timber, shallows (fishing), thickets (foraging), wolves, goblins, and the **Bog Horror** boss.
- **Dark Cave** — a descending dungeon.
- **Iron Peaks** — ore, gems, bandits, ogres, harpies.
- **Sunken Temple** — pay for a full restore.
- **Bandit Camp** and **Sunken Ruins** — unlocked later, home to the **Bandit Warlord** and the **Iron Warden**.

Travelling can trigger **random events** — ambushes, merchants, shrines, caches, caravans, duels. Events also happen on their own as you play, sometimes with big rewards.

## Quests

- **Main missions** are the five story chapters. Each ends with a **choice** that shapes the story — different choices unlock different side quests and outcomes, so a new run can go differently.
- **Side quests** open as the story progresses, plus **class missions** for your class and endless **bounties** in the Open World.
- Every quest lists its **objectives**, its **rewards**, and **what it unlocks**.
- If you **fail** a mission, a note appears telling you how to prepare for the next attempt — level, gear, potions, elements and tactics.

## Home, Forge & Market

- **Home** — grow your settlement from Camp to City, then build a **Farm, Mine, Tavern, Barracks, Workshop, Trade Post and Shrine**, and assign settlers to boost output.
- **Forge** — craft consumables and gear. Craft skill and a Workshop improve the rarity you roll.
- **Market** — buy and sell materials; prices move constantly. Reputation and a Trade Post improve prices.

## Activities (beyond fighting)

All of these are reachable from the **local map** in Oakhaven and elsewhere: fishing, foraging, farming, cooking, gambling at the **Dice Den**, the **Arena** (endless waves with rising rewards), the **Bounty Board**, tavern rumours for hints, mining, prospecting and woodcutting.

## The Underworld

Crime runs on **Nerve**, and success scales with Stealth, Finesse and class:

- **Pickpocket** (3) · **Lockpick** (2 + a lockpick) · **Heist** (8) · **Guild job** (4, needs renown).
- **Heat** rises with every crime. High Heat means the watch shakes you down as you travel. **Lay Low** (50g) cools it.
- The **Black Market** buys and sells contraband.

## The endgame

Finish the five chapters to unlock the **Open World**. Then chase **38 achievements**, the statistics page, and **Ascend** (New Game+) for permanent power — +1 to every attribute and bonus points per rank.

## Hints and shortcuts

- The **Hints** panel in the Story tab reads your situation and suggests what to do next — when to heal, where to spend points, and which element an upcoming boss fears.
- **Esc** closes any open window; **1–9** jump between tabs.

## Learning the game

- **Hover almost anything** — stats, items, enemies, skills, buttons — for a wiki explanation. (Toggle tooltips in Settings.)
- The **Wiki tab** has ~200 searchable entries covering every mechanic and object.
- The **bell** in the header holds your notification history for every event, quest and unlock.
- The **Codex** tracks achievements and statistics.

## Mods

The game looks for add-ons in the `mods/` folder automatically. Any it finds appear on the **new-game screen** and in **Settings → Mods**, where you can switch each one on or off.

- **Content mods** (new enemies, items, quests, languages) apply immediately.
- **Story and overhaul mods** change the narrative or rebalance the game, so they only take effect after you **restart** — this keeps an in-progress save safe.
- **Compatibility**: the Mods panel tells you which mods work together (✓), which overlap (⚠) and which cannot load (✗, with the reason). A broken mod is skipped so the game still runs.
- **Safe mode** (in Settings, or add `?safe=1` to the address) runs the game with no mods at all.

**Graphics packs** are mods that ship images. The game draws all its art through one function, so a pack can replace the emoji with sprites without any engine changes. Two packs ship with the game — **Flat Icons** (simple PNG icons) and **Chronicles Vector** (102 illustrated SVG models) — choose one in **Settings → Graphics pack**. Anything a pack doesn't cover keeps its emoji.

To add a mod, drop its `.js` file (or a folder with a `mod.js`) into `mods/`, run `python3 tools/build-mods-index.py`, and reload. Full instructions and the file format are in **mods/README.md**.

## Settings

Theme (Dark, Midnight Blue, Parchment, High Contrast), difficulty, text size, sound and volume, tooltips, notifications, damage numbers, screen shake, autosave, reduced motion, compact layout, and colourblind mode.

## Saving

Progress auto-saves to its slot. **Menu → Export Save** copies a backup string; **Import Save** restores it on another device.

*Tips: keep energy in reserve; sell materials when prices peak; Defend when you see ⚠️; match your element to the enemy's weakness; and grow your settlement early for a steady income.*
