## Making your own game with this engine

You are welcome to. The engine is MIT licensed. But you must supply **your own
art** — this repository's artwork is not part of the open-source grant. Delete
the art folders, drop in your own, and ship.

## The example art packs

`mods/example-graphics/` and `mods/overhaul-vector/` are generated by the scripts
in `tools/` from simple geometric shapes. Those *scripts* are MIT like the rest
of the engine; the images they produce are covered by this licence.
