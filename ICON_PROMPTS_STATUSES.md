# Aethelgard — icon prompts — status effects

The 10 status effects. They show as small chips on enemy and player cards during combat.

**10 icons. Generate each at 1:1, on a completely plain flat solid magenta
background** — that is what lets me cut them out. No floor, no shadow, no scene,
no second colour. Save each under the exact filename in its heading.

Everything here is optional: skip any icon and that entry keeps the emoji it
shows today. Send them in any batch size.

---

### Where these go

`status_<key>.png` becomes `status.<key>` — e.g. `status_bleed.png` fills `status.bleed`.
