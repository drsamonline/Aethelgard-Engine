# Aethelgard — icon prompts — achievements

The 39 achievements. They show as badges in the Codex.

**39 icons. Generate each at 1:1, on a completely plain flat solid magenta
background** — that is what lets me cut them out. No floor, no shadow, no scene,
no second colour. Save each under the exact filename in its heading.

Everything here is optional: skip any icon and that entry keeps the emoji it
shows today. Send them in any batch size.

---

### Where these go

`ach_<id>.png` becomes `ach.<id>` — e.g. `ach_first_blood.png` fills `ach.first_blood`.
